# Disputing an error on your credit report
## What to do
Get your reports from Equifax, Experian and TransUnion and find the incorrect item, such as an account that is not yours, a wrong balance or a wrong late payment. File a dispute with each credit bureau that shows the error, online or by certified mail. Explain what is wrong and attach proof such as statements or letters. Also send a dispute to the company that reported the information (the furnisher).
## Timelines and rights
The bureau generally must investigate within 30 days, or 45 days if you send extra information during the investigation. It must correct or delete information it cannot verify and send you the results in writing. If the item stays on your report, you can add a short statement of dispute to your file.
