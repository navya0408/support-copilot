# How long negative items stay on your credit report
## What to know
Most negative items, such as late payments, collections and charge-offs, can stay on your credit report for seven years. The seven years are counted from the date of first delinquency, not from the date a debt was sold or moved to a collector. Bankruptcies can stay for up to ten years depending on the type.
## What to do
Check the dates on each item. If an item is older than the legal reporting period, or the dates look wrong, dispute it with the credit bureau and attach proof. Paying a collection does not always remove it from your report immediately, but it should be shown as paid.
