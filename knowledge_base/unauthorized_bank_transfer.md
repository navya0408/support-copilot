# Unauthorized debit card, ACH or electronic bank transfers
## What to do
Tell your bank immediately by phone and in writing. Ask the bank to lock the card or account and to open an error investigation. Change your online banking passwords and review all recent transactions.
## Timelines and rights
Your liability is limited to 50 dollars if you report within two business days of learning that your card or access device was lost or stolen, and can be higher if you wait longer. You should report any unauthorized transfer within 60 days of the statement that shows it. The bank generally has 10 business days to investigate; if it needs up to 45 days it must usually give you a temporary credit for the disputed amount.
## Signs of an unauthorized transaction
Money taken from your checking or savings account without your permission, a debit card purchase you did not make, or an electronic payment you did not approve all count as unauthorized transactions. Report them to your bank right away.
