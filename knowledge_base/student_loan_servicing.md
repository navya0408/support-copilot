# Student loan servicing problems
## What to do
Check your loan details on StudentAid.gov and identify your servicer. If payments were misapplied or you were given wrong information, send a written complaint with proof to the servicer. Ask about income-driven repayment, deferment or forbearance if you cannot afford payments.
## Escalation
If the servicer does not fix the problem, contact the Federal Student Aid Ombudsman Group and submit a complaint to the CFPB. Keep records of every call, including the date, the representative's name and a reference number.
