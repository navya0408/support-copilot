# Hard inquiries you did not authorize
## What to do
A hard inquiry appears when a lender checks your credit after you apply. If you see one you did not authorize, contact the lender named on the inquiry and ask whether an application was made in your name. Dispute the inquiry with the credit bureau in writing. If you never applied, treat it as possible identity theft and place a fraud alert or freeze.
## Timelines and rights
Hard inquiries stay on your credit report for two years. Several inquiries for the same type of loan within a short shopping period are usually counted as one for scoring purposes.
