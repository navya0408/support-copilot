# Billing errors on a credit card
## What to do
If you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or did not receive the goods or services you paid for, send a written billing error notice to the address on your statement for billing disputes. Include your name, account number, the amount and why you think it is an error. Try to contact the merchant too.
## Timelines and rights
You must send the notice within 60 days after the statement with the error was mailed. The issuer must acknowledge your notice within 30 days and resolve the dispute within two billing cycles, and no later than 90 days.
