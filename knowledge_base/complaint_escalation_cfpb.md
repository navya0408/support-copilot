# Escalating a complaint to the CFPB
## What to do
First contact the company and note the date, the person you spoke with and a reference number. If the problem is not resolved, submit a complaint at consumerfinance.gov/complaint. Describe what happened, list the dates and amounts, say what resolution you want and attach documents.
## Timelines and rights
The CFPB forwards your complaint to the company. The company generally responds within 15 days, and in most cases resolves the complaint within 60 days. You can review the company's response and give feedback on whether the issue was resolved.
