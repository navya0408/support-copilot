# Fraud alerts and credit freezes
## What to do
A fraud alert tells lenders to take extra steps to verify your identity before opening credit. Contact one credit bureau and it must tell the other two. A credit freeze blocks lenders from viewing your credit report, which makes it hard for anyone to open new accounts in your name. Freezing and unfreezing is free, and you can lift a freeze temporarily when you apply for credit yourself.
## Timelines and rights
An initial fraud alert lasts one year. An extended fraud alert lasts seven years and is available to identity theft victims who have an identity theft report. A credit freeze stays in place until you remove it.
