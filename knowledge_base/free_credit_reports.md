# Getting your free credit reports
## What to do
You can get free credit reports from Equifax, Experian and TransUnion through AnnualCreditReport.com. Check all three, because each bureau may hold different information. Review the personal details, accounts, collections and inquiries, and note anything you do not recognize.
## Timelines and rights
Free reports are available weekly through AnnualCreditReport.com. You are also entitled to a free report if a company denies you credit or takes another negative action because of your report; ask for it within 60 days of the notice.
