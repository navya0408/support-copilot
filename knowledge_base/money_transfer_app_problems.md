# Problems with money transfer and payment apps
## What to do
Report the problem inside the app right away and also to the bank or card linked to it. Save screenshots, transaction IDs, usernames and dates. If the payment was unauthorized, ask the provider to open an error investigation in writing.
## What to know
If you were tricked into sending money yourself, such as in a scam, protections can be limited, but you should still report it immediately. Balances held inside a payment app are not always insured like bank deposits, so check the provider's terms. Unresolved issues can be reported to the CFPB.
