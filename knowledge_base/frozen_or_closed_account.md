# Bank account frozen or closed
## What to do
Ask the bank in writing why the account was frozen or closed, what documents it needs to release it, and when your remaining balance will be returned. Keep records of every call and letter. Ask to speak with a supervisor or the bank's complaints department if you do not get a clear answer.
## What to know
Accounts are often frozen for fraud checks, legal orders or missing identity information. If you depend on the money for rent or bills, tell the bank so the case can be treated as urgent. If the problem is not resolved, submit a complaint to the CFPB.
