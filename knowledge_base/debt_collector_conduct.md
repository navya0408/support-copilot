# Debt collector harassment
## What is not allowed
Collectors may not call before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. in your time zone. They may not keep calling you at work after you say it is not allowed, use threats or abusive language, claim you will be arrested, or tell other people about your debt.
## What to do
Keep a log of dates, times and what was said, and save letters and voicemails. Send a written request to stop contact. Report violations to the CFPB, the FTC and your state attorney general. You may also have the right to sue, and the time limit to do so is generally one year from the violation.
