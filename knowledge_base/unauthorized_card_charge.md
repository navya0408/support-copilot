# A credit card charge you did not make
## What to do
Contact your card issuer immediately, report the charge as unauthorized, and ask for the card to be locked and replaced. Follow up in writing. Review the rest of your statement for other charges you do not recognize.
## Timelines and rights
Federal law limits your liability for unauthorized credit card charges to 50 dollars, and many issuers offer zero liability. While the issuer investigates, you do not have to pay the disputed amount. Report as soon as you notice the charge.
