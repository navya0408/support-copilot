# What to do if someone used your identity
## What to do
Report the theft at IdentityTheft.gov to get a personal recovery plan and an identity theft report. Place a fraud alert or credit freeze. Review all three credit reports and mark every account or inquiry you do not recognize. Contact each company where fraud happened, ask them to close or freeze the accounts, and dispute the fraudulent accounts in writing. File a police report if a company or creditor asks for one.
## Timelines and rights
Once a credit bureau accepts your identity theft report, it must block the fraudulent information within four business days. Keep copies of every letter, report number and the date you sent it.
## Signs of identity theft
You may be a victim if an account, loan or credit card was opened in your name without your knowledge, if you see accounts or inquiries you do not recognize on your credit report, or if someone used your name, Social Security number or other personal information without your permission.
