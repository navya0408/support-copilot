# Mortgage payment and servicing problems
## What to do
If a payment was misapplied or there is another servicing error, send a written notice of error to the address your servicer designates for it, with your loan number, a description of the problem and supporting documents. If you are struggling to pay, contact the servicer early and ask about loss mitigation options such as a repayment plan or loan modification. A HUD-approved housing counselor can help for free.
## Timelines and rights
The servicer must acknowledge a notice of error within five business days and generally resolve it within 30 business days. Servicers generally cannot begin foreclosure until a loan is more than 120 days delinquent.
