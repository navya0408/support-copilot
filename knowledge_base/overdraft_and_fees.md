# Overdraft and unexpected bank fees
## What to do
Read your account agreement and the fee schedule, and ask the bank for an itemized list of the fees charged. Ask the bank to reverse or waive the fee, especially if it is the first time or if a deposit was delayed. If you believe the fee was applied incorrectly, ask for a written explanation and escalate to a supervisor.
## Timelines and rights
For ATM and one-time debit card transactions, a bank generally needs your opt-in before charging overdraft fees. You can turn overdraft coverage off at any time. If the bank does not resolve the problem, submit a complaint to the CFPB.
