# Auto loan problems and repossession
## What to do
If you may miss payments, contact the lender before it happens and ask about deferment or a new payment plan. If your car was repossessed, ask for the repossession notice in writing and ask how you can get the vehicle back or retrieve personal belongings from it.
## What to know
Rules vary by state. Many states require the lender to send notice before selling the vehicle, and some allow you to reinstate the loan or redeem the car by paying what is owed. Do not ignore any letters, because deadlines are short. Disputes can be reported to the CFPB and your state attorney general.
